## Why Pitch Feedback Is Broken

Most founders don't get useful feedback on their pitch until it's too late — after a string of passes, a botched intro meeting, or a term sheet that never came.

The people who could give you the most useful feedback (investors) rarely do. They're busy, they're managing deal flow, and frankly, explaining why they passed isn't their job. Advisors and mentors mean well, but they're often too close to you or too far from the current fundraising environment to give you the signal you actually need.

Pitch coaches exist, but the good ones are expensive and hard to access. And most founder-facing feedback tools are generic — they tell you your slides need "more clarity" without telling you *what* an investor would actually do with what you've given them.
