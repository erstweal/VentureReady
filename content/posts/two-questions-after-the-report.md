This one we're especially proud of. We maintain a database of emerging VC funds — mostly Fund I and Fund II managers who closed new vehicles within the last 12 to 18 months. That detail matters more than most founders realize: a fund that just closed is actively writing first checks, and an emerging manager reads cold inbound in a way a name-brand firm simply doesn't. We refresh the database on a monthly cycle and verify every fund against public sources.

The Fund Match runs your company against that database and delivers the six funds whose published investment thesis best fits you — with the reasoning spelled out for each one. Check sizes. Whether they lead rounds or follow. A recommended order to approach them in. Even the near misses: funds that almost fit, and what would have to change for them to say yes.
