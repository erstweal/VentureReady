## Usage notes (not part of the email)

- **Which block:** A = Growth Roadmap led (evaluation found 3+ P1 gaps, "not angel-ready," no customer discovery, or a structurally mismatched ask). B = Fund Match led (ready or near-ready verdict, round ≥ $500K, or founder asked "who should I talk to?").
- **Personalization line is mandatory.** A delivery email with no evidence a human read the report kills the upsell.
- **Never** promise introductions to funds, success rates, or funding outcomes — the Fund Match is research, and the flat fee keeps it cleanly on the research side of the line.
- After purchase, Stripe redirects to /thank-you-roadmap or /thank-you-fundmatch, which tell the customer we work from their existing evaluation and to reply if anything changed.
